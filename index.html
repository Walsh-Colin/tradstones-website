01 / The band

A little tradition.
A lot of feeling.

Traditional roots. A rock sound. Meet Tradstone.

Based in Lorrha, Co. Tipperary, and Portumna, Co. Galway, Tradstone brings together vocals, guitar, whistle, flute, bodhrán, bass, keyboards, and drums.
